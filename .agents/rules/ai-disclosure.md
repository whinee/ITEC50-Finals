# AI Disclosure Rules

## General Disclosure

For every edit, after executing your response, always include the following statement in the **Back Matter** part, **AI Disclosure** chapter of the `paper/main.tex` file:

> In the interest of academic integrity, the following discloses the use of artificial intelligence tools during the development of this project. All architectural decisions, design choices, and implementation strategies were conceived, directed, and validated by the author. AI tools were used as assistive accelerators under the author's explicit instruction and supervision --- not as a substitute for understanding.
>
> \begin{itemize}
>   \item \textbf{HTML Templates}: The Jinja2 template files under \texttt{src/templates/bookmarks} were initially scaffolded with assistance from OpenAI Codex, and subsequently reviewed, corrected, and heavily modified by the author to conform to the application's architecture.
>   \item \textbf{Utility Scripts}: Several Python scripts in the \texttt{scripts/} directory were drafted with Anthropic's Claude AI as a starting point, with the author directing the logic, reviewing the output, and rewriting where necessary.
> \end{itemize}

and an **AI Disclosure** section in `docs/README.md`:

> In the interest of academic integrity, the following discloses the use of artificial intelligence tools during the development of this project. All architectural decisions, design choices, and implementation strategies were conceived, directed, and validated by the author. AI tools were used as assistive accelerators under the author's explicit instruction and supervision — not as a substitute for understanding.
>
> - **HTML Templates**: The Jinja2 template files under `src/templates/bookmarks` were initially scaffolded with assistance from OpenAI Codex, and subsequently reviewed, corrected, and heavily modified by the author to conform to the application's architecture.
> - **Utility Scripts**: Several Python scripts in the `scripts/` directory were drafted with Anthropic's Claude AI as a starting point, with the author directing the logic, reviewing the output, and rewriting where necessary.

And then, append to that section what you have done. Follow these additional formatting rules:
- **Framing**: Always frame AI contributions as assistive tools used under the author's direction. Use language like "drafted with AI assistance", "accelerated with AI pair-programming under the author's direction", or "authored with AI assistance to meet documentation standards". Do NOT use language like "generated by" or "AI generated and modified".
- **Formatting**: If the description of the task is getting massive, break it down and format it into a nested bulleted list (using `\begin{itemize}` for LaTeX and `-` for Markdown).
- **Naming**: Do NOT add ` (this session)` to the name of the task you completed. Just describe the name of the task clearly (e.g. `\textbf{Backend Architecture Documentation}`).
- **Closing Statement**: The LaTeX disclosure must end with a closing paragraph crediting the author with the overall architecture, design, and substantive content. Use the following as a template:

> The overall system architecture, the Johnny.Decimal integration model, the zero-trust security design, the CSS design system, the JavaScript interaction logic, the database schema, the deployment strategy, and the substantive content of the reflection and rationale were authored entirely by the author. The use of AI tools did not substitute for technical understanding; it accelerated execution of decisions already made.

- **Fairness**: Giving credit to developers of AI tools while accurately representing the author's directing role
