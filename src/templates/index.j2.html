The Johnny.Decimal Anchor

DeciMark is uniquely built around the Johnny.Decimal system. By restricting organization to a strict two-level flat hierarchy and numerical categorization, it prevents the cognitive overhead of infinitely nested folders, making search and retrieval incredibly fast and predictable.

Absolute Privacy

Your data belongs to you. DeciMark ensures a secure environment where your links, notes, and tags are accessible solely by you. No tracking, no data selling, just you and your resources.

Blazing Fast

Engineered for speed, DeciMark boasts a lightweight footprint with minimal client-side bloat. Whether you have ten bookmarks or ten thousand, your dashboard remains instantly responsive.
